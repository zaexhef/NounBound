# Content Bible

## Launch worlds

### Celebrity Spotlight
Actors, musicians, athletes, directors, stage names, famous families, roles, and awards. Avoid gossip and unsourced personal claims.

### Motor City Garage
Makes, models, parts, racing, designers, engines, classic vehicles, and cars named after animals or places.

### House of Objects
Tools, kitchens, clothing, electronics, furniture, workplaces, toys, materials, and objects with double meanings.

### History Vault
Leaders, inventions, civilizations, documents, landmarks, eras, movements, and chronological challenges.

## Signature crossovers

- Cars named after animals
- Celebrities sharing vehicle surnames
- Objects invented during wartime
- Historical figures portrayed on screen
- Musicians with geographic names
- Words that change meaning across all four launch worlds

## MVP content

Ten puzzles:
- 2 tutorial
- 3 easy
- 3 normal
- 1 hard
- 1 Category Collision showcase

Each needs four groups, four staged hints, four explanations, one completion fact, and editorial metadata.

## Launch content

85 puzzles distributed according to the roadmap. Each world should have a gentle difficulty curve and at least two signature crossover boards.

## Journey structure

Each world contains:
- Arrival/tutorial node
- 5–8 standard puzzle nodes
- Challenge node
- Restoration milestone
- World finale with a connection chain into the next world

World progress should remain understandable without a story-heavy script.

## Educational facts

Facts are optional rewards after solving. They must:
- Be brief and relevant
- Avoid overstating uncertain history
- Carry internal source notes
- Never be required to understand an earlier answer
- Be replaceable through content versioning

## Daily content

Not part of MVP. After backend introduction:
- One globally shared approved puzzle per local day
- Downloaded and cached in advance
- Offline fallback from a bundled reserve
- No loss of streak when service failure prevents access
- Corrections and retirement supported by version

## Content cadence after launch

- Daily Mystery: daily
- Small curated pack: monthly
- Seasonal cosmetic theme: optional
- New world: only when enough reviewed content exists
- Retire or revise puzzles based on player reports and solve data

## Representation

Build broad, accurate coverage across time periods, regions, cultures, disciplines, and identities. Diversity must appear throughout normal content rather than being isolated into a single token category.
