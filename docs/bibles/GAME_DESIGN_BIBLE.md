# Game Design Bible

## Vision

NounBound is a thoughtful, approachable mobile puzzle journey built around discovering the hidden relationship between ambiguous nouns.

## Design pillars

1. **Fair surprise:** Answers may be clever, never arbitrary.
2. **Meaningful ambiguity:** A noun may suggest several paths, but the complete board has one fair intended partition.
3. **Visible journey:** Solving boards restores themed locations and advances connection chains.
4. **Knowledge without punishment:** Explanations teach; obscure trivia does not dominate early play.
5. **Respect the player:** No forced timer or paid solution to unfair design.

## Core loop

Choose puzzle → inspect nouns → select four → submit → receive feedback → solve groups → read explanations → earn mastery rewards → advance.

## Classic rules

- 16 unique visible cards, shuffled without changing solution data.
- Four intended groups, each containing exactly four nouns.
- Players may select up to four cards.
- A correct submission locks the group and reveals its label.
- An incorrect submission consumes one of three mistakes.
- A “one away” message may appear when exactly three selected nouns belong to one unsolved group.
- Losing reveals the solution and allows replay; it does not consume energy.
- Naming a category is optional and awards bonus Insight Points.

## Scoring

Base: 1,000 points per solved board.

Bonuses:
- +250 no mistakes
- +150 no hints
- +100 per correctly named category
- +100 Category Collision bonus
- Time is tracked for personal statistics but does not reduce normal-mode score

Stars:
- 1 star: complete
- 2 stars: complete with no more than one hint or mistake
- 3 stars: complete with no hints and no mistakes

## Difficulty

- **Tutorial:** Demonstrates one mechanic at a time.
- **Easy:** Familiar nouns; direct labels; few overlaps.
- **Normal:** One or two strong overlaps; broad cultural knowledge.
- **Hard:** Multiple meanings and deceptive arrangements.
- **Expert:** Indirect/category-collision relationships, still explainable.
- **Master:** Player types category labels; optional late-game mode.

Difficulty comes from ambiguity and inference, not tiny factual details.

## Modes

- Classic Sort
- Category Collision
- Connection Journey
- Chain Builder
- Odd Noun Out
- Timeline Sort
- Reverse Puzzle
- Daily Mystery
- Optional asynchronous head-to-head after launch

## Hints

Progressive, one hint per request:
1. Reveal a broad subject for one unsolved group.
2. Lock one correct noun in that group.
3. Remove one deceptive candidate.
4. Reveal the group label.

Hints never spend premium currency in the MVP.

## Win/loss and interruption

- Win when all four intended groups are locked.
- Loss at three incorrect submissions.
- App interruption saves board order, solved groups, selections, mistakes, hints, and elapsed time.
- Content updates never invalidate an active saved board.

## Progression

Insight Points, stars, achievements, journey restoration, cosmetics, and statistics. Player power does not alter puzzle correctness.

## Non-goals for MVP

No multiplayer, energy, loot boxes, leaderboard pressure, generative puzzle publishing, mandatory account, backend dependency, or pay-to-win hints.
