# Monetization Bible

## Principle

NounBound earns through additional convenience and expression, never by making a puzzle unfair or withholding a required answer.

## Approved options after MVP validation

- Rewarded ad for an optional hint
- One-time ad removal
- Premium themed puzzle packs
- Cosmetic card backs and board themes
- Optional supporter bundle

## Prohibited patterns

- Energy required to play
- Loot boxes
- Paying to correct an invalid puzzle
- Forced ad after every board
- Hiding the only solution behind payment
- Fake countdowns, misleading buttons, or accidental purchases
- Competitive power or score multipliers sold for money

## Rewarded-ad rules

- Player initiates the ad.
- Clearly state the exact reward.
- If no ad is available, provide a reasonable free fallback.
- Frequency cap sessions.
- Never interrupt active reasoning with an unsolicited full-screen ad.
- Respect consent choices and age/regional requirements.

## Purchases

- Clearly label one-time versus recurring purchases.
- Prefer no subscription at launch.
- Restore purchases across supported devices/accounts.
- Do not remove previously purchased content.
- Test cancellation, network failure, duplicate callbacks, and refunds.

## Suggested launch sequence

1. Release beta with no monetization.
2. Validate retention and puzzle fairness.
3. Add rewarded hints behind a feature flag.
4. Add one-time ad removal.
5. Add premium packs only when the editorial pipeline can maintain quality.

## Metrics with guardrails

Track opt-in ad engagement, purchase conversion, puzzle completion, hint use, and exits. If monetization raises exits or hint dependence sharply, reduce placement. Revenue does not override fairness.
